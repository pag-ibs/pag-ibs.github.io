title: "[WORKSHOP] 2026 Venus observations/modeling workshop (Korea-Japan)"

On 21 August 2026, IBS members presented their work and discussed potential collaborations with Japanese colleagues during an hybrid workshop.<br>
This workshop, led by Dr Yeon Joo Lee and Dr Shohei Aoki, brought together more than 16 participants from Korea and Japan, with the aim of fostering new collaborations between the two countries.<br>
